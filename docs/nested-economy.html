Nested Economy

Every scale of the game runs the same small economy: people who need housing, food, energy and work. A home base balances its own. A sector is the sum of the home bases in it, and a nation is the sum of its sectors. Totals flow up as needs and surpluses; taxes, prices and services flow back down. That round trip is the loop.

One turn of the loop

How a single home ripples out and comes back

1 · Home baseBuild to house and feed people

Housing sets how many people can live in a base. People eat food, use energy and fill jobs; jobs are what make cash, oil and fuel.

2 · Home baseBalance comes out plus or minus

Each resource ends the hour as made minus used. A surplus can be sold or shipped. A shortfall stops the population growing until it's covered.

3 · SectorThe sector adds up its homes

Every home's balance is summed. Where the sector comes up short, its hall posts a delivery order with a reward. That is the needs board players already fill.

4 · SectorTrucks move the surplus

Homes with spare grain or power ship it to sectors that need it, on the main road (taxed) or the back road (smuggled). Every truck shows on the map.

5 · NationThe nation sees the whole picture

Sector totals add up to national supply and demand. That moves market prices, and every legal delivery pays the nation's tax.

6 · Back downPolicy lands on every home

Players in government set the tax rate and laws, and spend the treasury on services. Cheaper food or better power lets homes grow, which starts the next turn.

What already exists and what this adds

  • Already in the game: home buildings that produce (crop plots, oil pumps, solar panels, housing), the sector needs board with delivery rewards, trucks on the main or back road, the nation's tax, the market, and the Caretaker's rations and tithe.
  • New: a population per home base, capped by housing and kept growing only while food and energy are covered; each building's upkeep in food, energy and workers; sector and nation totals worked out from the homes, and needs orders generated from real shortfalls instead of at random.
  • Where it would show: a ledger card on the Home view, the same card for a sector when you tap its hall, and a nation card in Politics. One layout at every scale, so a player learns it once.
