# Image-to-3D concept set

These images were generated as isolated, transparent, three-quarter-view source art for TripoSR or a similar single-image reconstruction tool. They share bright studio lighting, readable silhouettes, connected geometry, and restrained faction lighting.

## Assets and final prompt direction

- `enemy-leviathan-assault-bot.png` — wide hovering siege machine; central red optic; two integrated five-barrel cannon arms; dorsal power pylons; gunmetal armor; no legs or animal anatomy.
- `enemy-security-robot.png` — humanoid AI facility defender; disciplined rifle-ready stance; angular articulated armor; red visor and restrained enemy illumination.
- `enemy-shield-generator.png` — stable hexagonal infrastructure unit; three emitter pylons; central red core; conduits, vents, and an obvious weak point.
- `enemy-defense-turret.png` — rotating armored pedestal; twin autocannon clusters; recoil mechanisms, targeting optic, and sensor pods.
- `alliance-assault-soldier.png` — enclosed near-future infantry; practical layered armor; carbine in low-ready stance; graphite, tan, and amber alliance palette.
- `caretaker-support-drone.png` — hovering pearl-white support drone; protected optic; three compact utility arms; cyan and soft-magenta Caretaker illumination.
- `alliance-breach-rover.png` — low six-wheel indoor breach vehicle; hydraulic ram, cutter arm, cable spool, and sensor mast.
- `enemy-facility-power-core.png` — armored reactor pedestal; visible contained red plasma; structural braces, conduits, access panels, and shutdown interface.

## Shared constraints

Polished contemporary 3D game-asset concept; full object centered in a front three-quarter view; bright neutral lighting; transparent background; no scenery, floor, text, logos, smoke, effects, or watermark; all parts visibly connected and suitable for single-image 3D reconstruction.

## Suggested reconstruction order

Start with the power core, shield generator, turret, and rover. Their compact, mostly rigid silhouettes are more likely to reconstruct cleanly than a humanoid or the highly detailed Leviathan. The generated mesh should still be treated as a blockout: repair topology, separate moving assemblies, retopologize, and bake materials in Blender before game use.
