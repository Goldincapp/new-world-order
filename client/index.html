Reconstruction begins in Region 1
Rebuild what was lost. Decide what comes next.
Press anywhere to start the tour · sound on

Collect and upgrade

Press one of your gold-bordered oil parcels. The camera flies in and shows its 9 building slots. Press Collect and watch your oil count rise.

Sell on the market

Open the Market and sell 100 oil. Your cash goes up, 2% goes to the treasury, and the price drops a little each time you sell.

Scout your rivals

Press the town to the southwest. It belongs to the rival Blue Party, so the card offers Scout and Attack instead of Collect.

Survey your nation

Open Nation to see a whole region: nations named by players, sea lanes, a pipeline, a war front, a militia camp and a strait toll. Fog hides what your nation cannot see, and locked regions open in later seasons.

Visit home and the world

Home shows your walled HQ, barracks and trade office. World shows shipments moving along trade routes between countries.
